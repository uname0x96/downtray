Privacy Policy

Last updated September 24, 2026

Downtray does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. The app runs entirely on your Mac.

What the app reads

Downtray reads the folders you allow it to watch: Downloads by default, and others only when you add them in Settings. It reads them to show you the files there and to carry out the actions you ask for, such as opening, moving or trashing a file. Nothing is read outside those folders.

What the app stores

Settings, the list of watched folders, rules and the file history are stored locally in the app's sandbox container on your Mac. They never leave your computer. You can delete all of it by removing ~/Library/Containers/app.downtray.mac.

Network

Downtray makes no network connections. It has no analytics, no crash reporting of its own, no accounts and no advertising.

Purchases

The Pro upgrade is sold through the Mac App Store. Apple handles payment and receipts. The app only learns whether the purchase is present, and does not receive your name, email or payment details. Apple's handling of your data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Downtray does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
