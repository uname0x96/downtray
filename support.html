Support

Downtray is made by one person. Questions usually get an answer within a day or two.

Get in touch

Email uname0x96@gmail.com. Include your macOS version and the Downtray version from the gear menu if you are reporting a problem.

Bug reports and feature requests are also welcome on GitHub Issues.

Frequently asked questions

Downtray shows nothing after launch

Click the tray icon in the menu bar or press ⌃⌥D. On first launch macOS asks "Downtray would like to access files in your Downloads folder". Allow it. If you clicked Don't Allow, open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Files and Folders, find Downtray, and turn on Downloads Folder.

I do not see the menu bar icon

Downtray has no Dock icon. Look for the tray icon at the top right of the screen. If the menu bar is full, macOS hides icons from the left; close another menu bar app or use a wider display.

How do I change the shortcut?

Open Settings from the gear icon in the popover, then record a new shortcut under General → Show inbox.

I bought Pro on another Mac. How do I unlock it here?

Open Settings → Pro and click Restore Purchases. You need to be signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

I want a refund for Pro

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

A rule moved a file somewhere I did not expect

Open History (Pro) to see where every file went. Rules only act on the folders you watch and with the actions you configured. Edit or disable a rule under Settings → Rules.

A file is gone from the list but still in Downloads

The inbox shows files from the last 7 days by default. Change "Keep items" under Settings → List, or open History to find older files.

How do I remove everything Downtray stored?

Quit Downtray and delete the folder ~/Library/Containers/app.downtray.mac. This removes settings and history. Your files are never touched.
